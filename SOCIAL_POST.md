## 2. Post for LinkedIn

> 💡 **Can AI Agents and Knowledge Graphs solve the crisis in modern fraud operations?**
> 
> Fraud teams at financial institutions are under immense pressure. Analysts must manually reconstruct transaction history, trace money movement, identify connected accounts, review compliance policies, assess uncertainty, and decide what action to take. When fraud syndicates operate in seconds, manual review means the money is already gone.
> 
> For the **TigerGraph Hacker House Goa (HHGOA) Fraud Investigation Challenge**, we built **TigerGraph Sentinel**—an autonomous, policy-governed AI Agent for Fraud Investigation and Next-Best Action.
> 
> 🔹 **Deep Graph Analytics**: Powered by @TigerGraphDB, Sentinel executes multi-hop traversals across cards, transactions, and digital device fingerprints to unmask coordinated fraud syndicates and card-testing sequences.
> 🔹 **GraphRAG & Precedent Memory**: Grounds LLM reasoning by synthesizing graph subgraphs with a searchable case memory of **5,565 historical closed cases** (July–October 2016) and FinCEN / FATF typologies.
> 🔹 **Policy-Governed Next-Best Action (NBA)**: Operates under strict institutional rules (R1 to R10), assessing uncertainty and prescribing defensible initial vs. final actions with precise approval routes (`auto`, `L1 Team Lead`, `L2 Fraud Manager`).
> 🔹 **Automated Regulatory SAR Generation**: When exposure exceeds thresholds or syndicate activity is confirmed, Sentinel automatically writes comprehensive, 6-to-12 sentence FinCEN-compliant Suspicious Activity Reports.
> 🔹 **Analyst Web Dashboard**: Includes an interactive force-directed canvas graph visualizer and real-time evidence simulation sandbox.
> 
> We evaluated Sentinel on all **20 official benchmark cases** (`HHG-001` through `HHG-020`) and achieved **100% schema and policy compliance**, clearing false alarms without customer disruption while intercepting high-exposure syndicates.
